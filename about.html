About the studio

Passionate wedding storytellers with a cinematic eye

Krishna Photography preserves real emotion, graceful rituals, family warmth, and the quiet in-between moments that make your wedding truly yours.

Our philosophy

Every wedding deserves honesty, artistry, and care

We are a team of photographers, cinematographers, editors, and album designers who believe wedding coverage should feel effortless on the day and unforgettable for decades.

Our style blends emotional storytelling with polished editorial composition. We focus on real smiles, family blessings, sacred rituals, bridal details, groom portraits, decor, and celebrations without interrupting the natural flow of your day.

With high-end camera bodies, fast lenses, stabilizers, professional lighting, drone coverage, and color-accurate editing systems, we deliver premium photographs and cinematic films that feel rich, warm, and timeless.
