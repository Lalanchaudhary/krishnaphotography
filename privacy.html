Privacy and terms

Clear policies for a trustworthy booking experience

This policy explains how Krishna Photography handles booking details, payments, customer data, cookies, sharing, cancellations, and service terms.

Information Collection

We collect only the details needed to answer enquiries, prepare quotes, confirm bookings, and deliver photography services.

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Event date, venue, city, and service preference
  • Messages, creative references, and package requirements

Booking Information Usage

Your booking information is used to check availability, plan team schedules, prepare contracts, coordinate events, and deliver photos, films, and albums.

  • Event planning and communication
  • Package quotation and invoice preparation
  • Delivery updates and post-event support

Payment Security

Payments are handled through trusted banking or payment channels. We do not store sensitive card or banking credentials on this website.

  • Advance payments confirm date availability
  • Receipts may be shared by email or WhatsApp
  • Payment disputes are handled through documented communication

Customer Data Protection

We keep client information private and use reasonable safeguards to protect booking details, contact information, and delivered media.

  • Project folders are shared only with authorized team members
  • Client galleries may use private links
  • Data access is limited to service delivery needs

Cookies Usage

This static website may use basic browser storage or third-party embeds such as maps, fonts, or analytics if enabled in the future.

  • Cookies may improve performance and browsing experience
  • Third-party services may apply their own cookie policies
  • You may control cookies in your browser settings

Third-Party Sharing Policy

We do not sell client information. Details may be shared only with trusted team members, album vendors, editors, or logistics partners when necessary.

  • No sale of personal data
  • Limited sharing for project delivery
  • Legal sharing only when required by applicable law

Terms of Service

Bookings are subject to confirmed dates, agreed deliverables, payment schedule, event access, travel arrangements, and mutual cooperation.

  • Final deliverables depend on the selected package
  • Clients must provide accurate event schedules
  • Creative editing decisions remain with the studio

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Advance booking amounts reserve team availability and may be non-refundable once a date is blocked. Rescheduling is considered based on availability.

  • Cancellation terms are shared before confirmation
  • Refunds depend on work completed and date proximity
  • Force majeure cases are reviewed fairly

Media Usage Permission

With client permission, selected photos or films may be used in portfolio, website, social media, and promotional material.

  • Private events can request non-public delivery
  • Portfolio usage can be discussed before booking
  • Sensitive moments are handled respectfully
